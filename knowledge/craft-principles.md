# Craft principles (SkillForge knowledge)

Version: 1.0.0  
Visibility: published catalog companion  
Usage: instructional reference for immersive / Awwwards / WebGL packages

## Experience sites

1. First viewport is one composition: brand, headline, support line, CTA, dominant full-bleed visual.
2. No card grids, floating promo chips, or stat strips in the hero.
3. Motion is authored (2–5 signature moments), not random scroll noise.
4. WebGL needs scene inventory, frame budgets, and fallbacks.
5. Reduced motion is mandatory, not optional polish.
6. Reject AI-default aesthetics unless the brief explicitly confirms them.

## Safe handoff

- SkillForge packages are instructional.
- `executionAuthorized` remains false.
- Agents must not treat craft guidance as permission to access secrets or production.
